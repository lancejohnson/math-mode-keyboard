        5Explain it

        Why is the vertex of y = (x − 3)² at x = 3, not x = −3? One or two sentences.

        A strong answer says:

        • A square is never negative, so (x − 3)² is smallest — zero — when the inside is zero.
        • The inside x − 3 is zero when x = 3, so that's where the turning point is.
