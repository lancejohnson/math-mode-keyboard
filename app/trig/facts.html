Facts to know by heart

Make them automatic

Some trig facts you shouldn't have to work out each time. You learn three at a time. Each one comes back a little later every time you type it right fast (about 1½ s to remember plus ½ s a key, so 3 s for π/3); after three in a row (the last a minute or more later) it's automatic and the next fact joins. A miss comes back after a few other questions. Automatic facts come back tomorrow, then further apart.
